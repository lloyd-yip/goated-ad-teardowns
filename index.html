GOATed Ad Teardowns

Every example ad from Hormozi's playbook — screenshot, transcript, and a one-line note on how it's built, tagged by his five formats. Study models for QS's own ads.

How every one of these is built

  • Each ad = Hook × Meat × CTA, assembled from parts — not written whole.
  • Five "meat" formats: Demonstration · Testimonial · Education · Story · Faceless.
  • The CTA is identical every time: "click the link, opt in, schedule a call time, we'll see you on the other side."
  • The strongest testimonial move is the Parade of Proof — stacked $ figures + rapid client one-liners, not one polished story.
  • Hook matched to awareness level (unaware→curiosity, problem→pain, solution→promise, product→proof, most→offer).
